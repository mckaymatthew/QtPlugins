* This outlines some simple other game based plugins that could be made. 

* Mustand and Mayonanse
  * In this game the player has to work very late to meet an upcoming flight test at work then must drive home.
  * The game is in 3 phases
  * The first phase, at work
    * At work they are in front of a computer with a keyboard. On the desk are out of focus are circuit cards and wires.
    * They have to type in some C++ lines of code. As they type, they bet blurrier and blurrier until its unreadable.
    * When it is unreadable a button "go home" appears.
  * Second phase, driving home
    * The player is behind a wheel of a car driving down a road.
    * The user has to use the left and right keys to keep a car between the yellow dashed lines and the white line on the right
    * Occasionally their vision must blur and their steering is slightly distrubed
    * If they drift across the yellow line it must say "get out the mustard". Similar, if they drift across the white it says "say off the mayo"
    * There is a progress bar that increases as you drive. When it completes you hit phsae 3
  * Third phase
    * This has two options
       * Option 1, you arrive home, and click go to bed
       * Option 2, you arrive at what-a-burger, you click "eat", some time passes (and a progress bar for eating), then you can click "go back to work"
